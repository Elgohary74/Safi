How do I create a group?

Simply click on the "Create Group" button on your dashboard or the "My Groups" page. Enter a name and description, and you're good to go!

How do I add expenses?

Navigate to a group's detail page and click "Add Expense". You can specify the amount, description, and who paid.

Is Safi free to use?

Yes! Safi is completely free for managing shared expenses with friends and family.

Can I settle debts through the app?

We record settlements, but the actual money transfer happens outside the app (e.g., via InstaPay, Cash, or Bank Transfer). You just mark it as "Settled" here.
