Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a group?

Simply sign up, go to your dashboard, and click the "Create Group" button. Give it a name and you're ready to add members!

How do I add expenses?

Navigate to a specific group's page and click "Add Expense". Enter the amount, description, and who paid. You can now also select specific members to split the cost with.

Is Safi free to use?

Yes! Safi is completely free for managing shared expenses with friends and family.

Can I settle debts through the app?

We record settlements to update balances, but the actual money transfer happens outside the app (e.g., via InstaPay, Cash, or Bank Transfer). Just click "Settle Up" to record it.
